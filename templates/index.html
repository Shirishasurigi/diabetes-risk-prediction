Key EDA Clinical Takeaways

🔍
Biologically Invalid Zeros

Insulin (48.7%), SkinThickness (29.6%), and Glucose/BMI exhibit zero values representing missing clinical data, necessitating rigorous median imputation.

🩸
Glucose Primacy

2-Hour Plasma Glucose exhibits the highest individual correlation (r = +0.467) with diabetes manifestation, showing significant separation between classes.

⚖️
BMI & Metabolic Syndrome

BMI (r = +0.293) and Age (r = +0.238) demonstrate compounding risk: patients with BMI > 30 and age > 35 show more than triple the diabetes incidence.

🧬
Pedigree & Heredity

Diabetes Pedigree Function demonstrates non-linear escalation of risk, especially when coupled with elevated postprandial glucose.
